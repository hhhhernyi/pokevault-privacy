Privacy Policy

PokéVault for iOS · last updated 25 September 2026

The short version

PokéVault keeps an account so your collection follows you between devices. It stores what you tell it about your cards and nothing else. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What the app collects

  • Account: your email address and a sign-in credential. If you sign in with Apple or Google, PokéVault receives the email address (or Apple's private relay address) that the provider shares, and never sees your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile: the display name, country and currency you choose during setup. These personalise the app; the currency is the default for purchase prices.
  • Your collection: which cards you own and, for each copy, anything you choose to record — variant, condition, grading company and grade, and purchase price.

What is not collected

  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access. The app never asks for these permissions.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No usage analytics or third-party crash reporting.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to sign you in, and to show you your own collection and its completion. Nobody else can see your collection. Your data is not used for advertising, profiling, or anything unrelated to the app.

Where it is stored

  • Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Your profile and collection are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon on Amazon Web Services in Singapore, and reached only through PokéVault's own server, which checks that each request comes from the signed-in account it concerns.
  • On your device, the app keeps a copy of recently viewed data, and any changes made while offline, so it works without a connection. Logging out or deleting your account clears it.

Card images

Card and set images are loaded from TCGdex, a public Pokémon card database. As with any website, TCGdex's servers see the network address of the device requesting an image. No account information is sent to them.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. After you confirm your identity, your profile, your entire collection and your sign-in account are permanently deleted, and for Sign in with Apple accounts the app's access to your Apple ID is revoked. This cannot be undone. If you cannot access the app, email the address below and the account will be deleted for you.

Children

PokéVault is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be deleted.

Changes

If what the app collects changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
